# Reference years in selector labels

Researched on 6 November 2026. The five formerly undated labels now display
**reference publication years**, explicitly followed by `reference`. A dated
reference is not proof that the repository's exact polygon transcription came
from that edition. Geometry, class names, export IDs and categories are unchanged.

| View | Displayed year | Evidence and meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New Zealand | 1995 reference | Milne, Clayden, Singleton and Wilson, *Soil Description Handbook*, Manaaki Whenua Press; the publisher's catalogue dates this edition to 1995. |
| USDA | 1951 reference | Soil Survey Staff, *Soil Survey Manual*, Agriculture Handbook 18; USDA ARS reproduces the 12-class system with an explicit 1951 attribution. |
| Belgium | 1984 reference | Verheye and Ameryckx, *Mineral fractions and classification of soil texture*; a Ghent University paper explicitly attributes its seven-class Belgian triangle to this 1984 reference. |
| France, Aisne/Jamagne | 1967 reference | Jamagne, *Bases et techniques d'une cartographie des sols*; INRAE explicitly cites 1967 and describes subsequent modifications. |
| Australia | 2018 reference | Pluske, Boggs and Leopold, *Soil Quality: 2 Integrated Soil Management*; SoilsWest explicitly dates the Australian reference diagram to this 2018 book. |

## Evidence links

- New Zealand: [publisher's handbook catalogue](https://cdm20022.contentdm.oclc.org/digital/collection/p20022coll14/id/79/),
  [permanent DOI](https://doi.org/10.7931/DL1JG6).
- USDA: [USDA ARS / NRC report, Figure A1, PDF page 158](https://www.ars.usda.gov/arsuserfiles/20361500/pdf_pubs/P2203.pdf#page=158).
  Its caption identifies the USDA triangle as Soil Survey Staff (1951).
- Belgium: [Van Meirvenne, Predictive Quality of the Belgian Soil Survey Information,
  Figure 1 and references](https://www.ugent.be/bw/environment/en/research/orbit/Recent-publications/pdf/pdf2007-ouder/BBV-SIS.pdf).
  The paper is published in 1998 but its triangle attribution is 1984; the
  selector uses the cited triangle reference year, not the hosting paper's year.
- Aisne/Jamagne: [INRAE, Figure 2 and Jamagne bibliography entry](https://mots-agronomie.inrae.fr/index.php?title=Granulom%C3%A9trie_et_diagrammes_de_texture).
- Australia: [SoilsWest, Laboratory method and reference list](https://soilqualityknowledgebase.org.au/soil-texture/).
  The page was updated in 2024; the credited book and diagram reference are
  2018. Neither date establishes when the Australian scheme originated.

## Limits of this update

The original New Zealand, USDA and Australian polygon arrays still lack
verified transcription provenance. This date research does not certify their
scientific boundaries or resolve the documented Australian overlap.

The Australian published reference figure has different boundaries from the
legacy arrays, including the silty-loam/clay-loam area. **2018 is only the date
of a relevant published reference, not a verified year for the implemented
Australian polygon variant.** The selector marks it as such. A precise
edition attribution for that variant remains unresolved.

For Aisne, 1967 is the foundational Jamagne reference, not a verified date for
every later modification in the implemented 15-class view. Belgium's 1984
reference is a documented publication of the triangle, not a claim that the
scheme originated in 1984.

Independent scientific verification and package comparisons remain future
work. When an exact implemented edition is established, its year can replace
the qualified reference date with supporting documentation.
